# 0003 — Preserve CI-outage evidence without waiving protection

Status: Accepted (2026-10-05), safe evidence subset only.

## Context

DevStandard ADR 0025 and its amendments require the merging main session's full
synthetic-merge evidence, public independent audit and service-return sweep. A
protected branch's historical exception belongs to the human. The target guard
refuses fallback admission, and an enforced Actions-app check cannot be supplied
by local evidence. Current product protection founding is separately unresolved.

Issue 17's whole independent pre-code challenge initially requested separate
unmerged-PR return and actual degraded-merge recovery, an effective release-hold
consumer and a smaller design. The revised guide plus focused collector was accepted
at spec blob `a199791f49d3e9fab32702e7f20b1fc916cc8ef0`; header-only accepted blob
`2d2e4521c0f9db7d029f88ca8c0a44d2132b414e` was published before implementation.
The root authorized that safe subset, not a human waiver or product integration.

## Decision

Ship guidance and one focused evidence collector with a reviewed, byte-pinned map
for the current PR workflow. Retain both named external causes, exact ordered
synthetic parents/tree, whole original outputs, input/setup boundaries, publication
recovery and fresh independent helper audit. Unsupported semantics and incapable
jobs refuse full equivalence. The collector never grants operational cause
qualification or merge permission; cause authenticity remains independently audited.

Reuse existing digest/durable-save primitives without formal acceptance records.
The seven-command lifecycle and outage ledger were rejected as disproportionate.
Waiting and ordinary protected CI remain the integration route. Safe temporary
self-hosted Actions are separate ordinary checks. A historical human-owned exception
still requires explicit new policy direction and its own decision before implementation.

The integrating session performs the documented release-hold inspection before any
tag/release action. Clear only against cause return, complete green ordinary
post-return exact-current-main CI and verified approved protection. No machine
release gate is claimed. Evidence-only return resumes exact-current-PR CI/review;
actual degraded-merge recovery remains conditional on future policy and real
integrated identities. Green main never certifies an unmerged PR.

## Consequences

Useful evidence can survive an outage without fabricating checks or weakening
protection. Whole carriers, explicit transport differences and pending publication
intents cost storage and audit effort. This is a partial migration; Issue 17's full
degraded-integration goal remains unresolved. Linux/native qualification, live
publication/recovery, protected refusal, ordinary exact-head CI, formal Goal/Floor
review and human merge authority remain separate obligations.

The root reserved 0003 above Issue 16's 0002; Issue 18 reserved 0004. Existing ADR
bodies remain unchanged.
